Troubleshooting Guide

×

If you're stuck, follow these steps carefully:

  1. Chrome Version: Ensure you are on Chrome 127 or newer. Go to `chrome://settings/help`.
  2. Enable Flags: Go to `chrome://flags`, find and **Enable** both `#prompt-api-for-gemini-nano` and `#optimization-guide-on-device-model`.
  3. Relaunch Chrome: After enabling flags, click the "Relaunch" button at the bottom of the `chrome://flags` page.
  4. Download Model: Go to `chrome://components`. Find "Optimization Guide On-Device Model" and click "Check for update". Wait for it to download (it's ~600MB).
  5. Restart Again: After the model downloads, **completely close and reopen Chrome**.
  6. Re-check: Return to this setup page and click "Re-check Status".
